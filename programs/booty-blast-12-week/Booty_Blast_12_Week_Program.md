## 2. The Science (Brett Contreras / Glute Lab principles applied)

1. **Train all three load vectors.** Glutes respond to vertical (squats, lunges), horizontal (thrusts, bridges, back extensions) and lateral (abduction) loading. Each day prioritises one but trains all.
2. **Train all muscle lengths.** Lengthened (RDLs, deep squats, deficit lunges) → Monday. Shortened/peak contraction (thrusts, bridges, kickbacks) → Wednesday. Heavy full-range → Friday. Deep-range squats grow glutes more than shallow ones (Kubo et al., 2019), and training muscles at long lengths is a strong growth driver (Maeo et al., 2021; Pedrosa et al., 2022).
3. **Use the whole rep spectrum.** Heavy (5–8), moderate (8–15) and high (15–30) reps. Glutes are a mixed-fibre muscle and respond to all of them when sets are taken close to failure.
4. **Frequency + volume.** 3 glute days and ~40 hard sets a week is a specialisation dose — higher than general training, appropriate for a 12-week focus block for trained clients (see Scaling in section 9).
5. **Hip thrusts AND squats.** Both build glutes about equally (Plotkin et al., 2023, co-authored by Contreras). That's why squats and thrusts both feature every day.
6. **Progressive overload, with good reps.** Load, reps, range, tempo or control must improve over time — but only with the same technique standard.
7. **Glute-dominant technique.** Posterior pelvic tilt and chin tuck at the top of thrusts, slight forward lean on split squats/lunges, push knees out, round the upper back on back extensions, drive through the heel/midfoot.
8. **Mind-muscle connection** matters most on lighter/isolation work — so Wednesday is the "feel it" day.

## 3. Tempo, Effort & Progression

### Tempo key (same as before)
**Eccentric – Bottom Pause – Concentric – Top Pause**, in seconds. Example `3-1-1-0` = 3 s down, 1 s pause, 1 s up, no pause at the top. Hip thrust `2-1-1-3` = 2 s lower, 1 s pause at the bottom, drive up, 3 s squeeze.

### RIR (Reps In Reserve)
RIR 2 = you could have done 2 more good reps. "Technical failure" = the next rep would break form.

| Week | Phase | Main lifts | Accessories | Isolation / abduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build | RIR 3 — find your weights | RIR 3 | RIR 2 |
| 2 | Build | RIR 2 | RIR 2 | RIR 1 |
| 3 | Build | RIR 1–2 | RIR 1 | Technical failure |
| **4** | **PR WEEK #1** | **Test** | RIR 1 (one set less on PR days) | Technical failure |
| 5 | Develop | RIR 2 (new variations) | RIR 2 | RIR 1 |
| 6 | Develop | RIR 1–2 | RIR 1 | Technical failure |
| 7 | Develop | RIR 1 | RIR 1 | Failure + intensity technique |
| **8** | **PR WEEK #2** | **Test** | RIR 1 (one set less on PR days) | Technical failure |
| **9** | **RELOAD or REMIX** | See section 6 | | |
| 10 | Peak | RIR 1–2 | RIR 1 | Failure + intensity technique |
| 11 | Peak | RIR 1 | RIR 0–1 | Failure + intensity technique |
| **12** | **FINAL PR WEEK** | **Test** | RIR 1 (one set less on PR days) | Technical failure |

### Double progression (how to add weight)
1. Work inside the rep range at the prescribed tempo and RIR.
2. When **all sets hit the top of the range** with clean reps → add load next session.
   - Hip thrust / belt squat / RDL: **+2.5–5 kg**
   - Dumbbells: **+1–2 kg** per hand
   - Machines / cables: **+1 pin / plate**
3. Restart at the bottom of the range and build again.
4. Can't add load? Add a rep, slow the eccentric, or deepen the range. **Progress is anything that's better than last time with the same technique.**

### Intensity techniques (Weeks 7, 10, 11 — last set of isolation work only)
- **Lengthened partials:** after failure, 5–10 partial reps in the stretched half.
- **Rest-pause:** after the last rep, rest 15 s, do max reps, rest 15 s, max reps again.
- **Drop set:** drop the load ~25% and go to technical failure again.
- **Mechanical drop (abduction machine):** lean forward → sit upright → lean back, no rest.

## 5. PR Weeks (Weeks 4, 8, 12)

Each glute day has **one designated PR lift**. The rest of the session runs as normal but with **one set removed from each accessory that has 3 sets** (keeps it ≤60 min and gives a built-in mini-taper).

| Day | PR Lift | Test | Valid rep standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Barbell RDL | 8RM | Bar to mid-shin or below with a neutral spine, 3 s lowering, hips fully locked out |
| Wednesday | Barbell Hip Thrust | 5RM | Shins vertical at the top, posterior tilt, **1 s pause at lockout** |
| Friday | Belt Squat (sumo) | 5RM | Hip crease to knee height or below, controlled descent |
| Upper (B) | Pull-up | Max reps (bodyweight) — or lowest assistance for 5 clean reps | Chin over bar, full hang at the bottom |

### PR protocol (replaces that lift's working sets)
1. **Ramp:** 10 reps empty bar/light → 6 @ ~50% → 4 @ ~70% → 2 @ ~85% of the expected top set (rest 90 s–2 min).
2. **PR attempt:** up to **3 attempts** at the target rep count, 3–4 min rest. Stop when a rep breaks technique.
3. **Back-off set:** drop 15–20%, one set to RIR 1. Log it — it's a rep PR for next time.
4. Week 1: no test — record the working weights used. Week 4 is the first official PR.

### Make it fun
- **PR Bell / PR Board** in the gym — ring it, write it up.
- **Hip Thrust Club milestones:** 60 kg · 80 kg · 100 kg · 1× bodyweight · 1.5× bodyweight · 2× bodyweight.
- **Relative strength leaderboard:** load ÷ bodyweight, so every client can compete.
- **Week 12 bonus:** Bodyweight single-leg glute bridge, max reps/leg — compare left vs right.

## 6. Week 9 — RELOAD (if needed) or REMIX

Run this **readiness check** at the end of Week 8. Tick each that applies:
- [ ] Joints or tendons aching (hips, knees, low back)
- [ ] Main lift performance dropped two sessions in a row
- [ ] Soreness hanging around longer than 72 h
- [ ] Sleep worse than usual / high life stress
- [ ] Motivation is flat, sessions feel like a grind

**2 or more ticks → RELOAD.** 0–1 ticks → **REMIX**.

| | RELOAD Week | REMIX Week |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | Phase 2 sessions | Same structure, new exercises (below) |
| Sets | Halve every exercise (round up) → ~20 glute sets | Normal 40 sets |
| Load | ~70% of Week 8 working weights | Moderate, new movements |
| Effort | RIR 4 — leave feeling better than you came in | RIR 2, **no failure, no PRs** |
| Tempo | Keep it — practise perfect reps | Keep it |
| Finishers | Skip; replace with 10 min easy walk + mobility | "Booty Games" (below) |

**REMIX swaps (one-week novelty):** Kettlebell Swings (hinge) · Landmine Squat (squat) · Lateral/Cossack Lunge (lunge) · Single-Leg Hip Thrust off a box (thrust) · Banded Monster Walk + Fire Hydrant (abduction) · Sled Push/Drag (finisher).

**Booty Games (finisher, pairs):** Partner A does a bodyweight glute bridge hold while Partner B does 10 KB swings — swap. Most rounds in 6 min wins.

**Upper day in Week 9:** on a Reload, halve the sets and use ~70% loads; on a Remix, run it as normal.

**Floating reload:** if a client hits 3+ ticks earlier (e.g. Week 6), take the reload then and slide the remaining weeks back by one.

## 9. Coaching Notes

**Scaling the volume**
- **Advanced / 2+ years consistent:** run as written (40 sets).
- **Intermediate:** remove one set from every 3-set exercise → ~31 sets/week. Add them back in Phase 2 if recovery is good.
- **Returning / beginner:** use the intermediate version, stay at RIR 2–3 all of Phase 1, and skip the PR test in Week 4 (log a rep PR instead).

**Logging (non-negotiable)** — every client logs weight × reps for the ★ lifts and the first two exercises of each day. No log = no overload.

**Equipment swaps**
- No belt squat → Hack Squat (feet high & wide) or Barbell Back Squat (wide stance).
- No rotisserie pad → Smith Machine Hip Thrust or Hip Thrust Machine.
- No reverse RDL machine → Reverse Hyper on a bench or 45° Back Extension.
- No GHD / 45° bench → Cable Pull-Through.

**Pain rules** — sharp or joint pain = stop that exercise and swap to a similar pattern the client can do pain-free. Muscle burn = good. Lower back taking over thrusts/back extensions = reduce load and re-cue the posterior pelvic tilt.

**Recovery basics** — 1.6–2.2 g protein per kg bodyweight per day, 7–9 h sleep, 8–10k steps. Glutes grow between sessions, not during them.
