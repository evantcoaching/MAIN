==================== 00_PROGRAM_OVERVIEW ====================

BOOTY BLAST 2.0
12-Week Glute Specialisation Program
3 Glute Days (40 working sets/week) + 1 Upper Body Sculpt Day
Abs every session

A science-backed training system designed to maximise glute growth, strength and shape through strategic exercise selection, progressive overload and intelligent weekly periodisation.

WEEKLY STRUCTURE
• Monday = Booty Blast LENGTHENED (13 sets) - load the glutes in the stretched position
• Tuesday (or Thursday) = Upper Body Sculpt - A on odd weeks, B on even weeks
• Wednesday = Booty Blast SQUEEZE (13 sets) - horizontal hip extension, peak contraction
• Friday = Booty Blast POWER (14 sets) - heavy vertical hip extension, hardest session
• Saturday & Sunday = Recovery (8-10k steps, sleep, protein)

EVERY GLUTE SESSION TRAINS 5 PATTERNS
Thrust/Bridge • Squat • Lunge • Hinge • Abduction

12-WEEK PLAN
Weeks 1-3 = Phase 1 BUILD
Week 4 = PR WEEK #1
Weeks 5-7 = Phase 2 DEVELOP
Week 8 = PR WEEK #2
Week 9 = RELOAD or REMIX (based on your readiness check)
Weeks 10-11 = Phase 3 PEAK
Week 12 = FINAL PR WEEK

TEMPO KEY
4 numbers = Lower - Bottom Pause - Lift - Top Pause (in seconds)
Example 3-1-1-0 = 3 sec down, 1 sec pause, 1 sec up, no pause at top.

RIR (REPS IN RESERVE)
RIR 2 = you could have done 2 more good reps.
Technical failure = the next rep would break your form.

HOW TO PROGRESS
When you hit the TOP of the rep range on ALL sets with good form, add weight next session:
• Barbell / belt squat: +2.5-5kg
• Dumbbells: +1-2kg each hand
• Machines & cables: +1 pin/plate
Then build the reps back up again. LOG EVERY SESSION. No log = no overload.

Train with purpose. Focus on quality movement. Embrace progressive overload. Consistency builds stronger, fuller, more resilient glutes.

==================== 01_P1_MONDAY_LENGTHENED ====================

BOOTY BLAST MONDAY - LENGTHENED
Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4)
Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy

FOCUS
Today's session loads the glutes in their most lengthened position - deep in the hip. Training a muscle under tension while it's stretched is one of the strongest drivers of muscle growth. Slow the lowering phase, own the bottom position and make every rep count.

TODAY'S INTENT
Control the stretch. Own the tension. Growth happens where ya willing to stay uncomfortable.

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 1 = RIR 3 (find your weights)
Week 2 = RIR 2
Week 3 = RIR 1-2
Week 4 = PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Reverse Hypers x10
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Deficit Reverse Lunge x6/leg (3 sec down)
• Hip Hinge with Dowel x10

1. BARBELL RDL ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3 (working sets - don't include warm-up sets)
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 2.5 min
Cues
Push the hips back like closing a car door with your butt.
Soft knees, bar stays on the legs.
Feel the stretch, then drive the hips through.

2. LEG PRESS HIGH & WIDE STANCE (DEEP)
Sets: 3
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Weight in heels.
Knees towards chest - as deep as your low back stays flat.
Imagine dragging your feet down the platform.

3. DB DEFICIT REVERSE LUNGE (front foot on 5-10cm plate)
Sets: 2
Reps: 8-10/leg
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Long step back.
Slight forward lean, chest over front knee.
Push through the front heel.

4. BARBELL HIP THRUST - BOTTOM PAUSE (Rotisserie Pad)
Sets: 3
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 2-2-1-1
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Lower until the butt nearly touches the floor.
Hold 2 sec at the bottom WITHOUT relaxing.
Tuck pelvis, chin down, drive up.

5. SUPERSET
A1. SEATED HIP ABDUCTION MACHINE - LEAN FORWARD
Sets: 2
Reps: 15-20
Tempo: 2-1-1-2
↓
A2. CABLE LATERAL RAISE (upper sprinkle)
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15/arm
Tempo: 2-1-1-1
Rest: 60 sec after both
Cues
Abduction: hinge forward over your thighs, pause wide. Last set - finish with 10 partial reps in the stretched (inner) half.
Lateral: lead with the elbow.

6. DECLINE SIT UP (full stretch at the bottom)
Sets: 2
Reps: To technical failure
Rest: 60 sec
Cues
Let the abs stretch over the bench.
Curl ribs to hips.
Focus on the breath. Use arm swing for assistance when needed.

7. FINISHER - ASSAULT BIKE TABATA
20 sec max / 10 sec easy x 8 rounds (4 min)
