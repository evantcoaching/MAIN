WEEK 9 - RELOAD OR REMIX
(Paste into the Week 9 workout instructions or send as a client message at the end of Week 8)

You've just finished 8 big weeks. Before Week 9, run this READINESS CHECK. Tick each that applies:
☐ Joints or tendons aching (hips, knees, low back)
☐ My main lifts went backwards two sessions in a row
☐ Soreness is hanging around longer than 72 hours
☐ Sleep is worse than usual / life stress is high
☐ Motivation is flat - sessions feel like a grind

2 OR MORE TICKS = RELOAD WEEK
0-1 TICKS = REMIX WEEK

RELOAD WEEK
• Run your Phase 2 workouts (Mon / Wed / Fri / Upper A)
• HALVE the sets on every exercise (3 sets = 2, 2 sets = 1)
• Use about 70% of your Week 8 weights
• RIR 4 - leave feeling better than when you walked in
• Keep the tempo - practise perfect reps
• Skip the finishers. Replace with a 10 min easy walk + mobility
A reload isn't going backwards. It's how you come back stronger for the Peak phase.

REMIX WEEK
• Run the REMIX workouts (Mon / Wed / Fri) - new exercises for one week
• Upper day = Upper A Phase 3 as normal
• RIR 2 - no failure, no PR attempts
• Have fun with it
