BOOTY BLAST WEDNESDAY - SQUEEZE
Phase 3 PEAK (Weeks 10-12)
Horizontal Hip Extension • Peak Contraction • Mind-Muscle Connection

FOCUS
Your heaviest paused hip thrusts of the program, a high-rep pump and big squeezes. Every rep should feel like the glutes are doing ALL the work.

TODAY'S INTENT
Squeeze with purpose. Feel every rep. If you can't feel it, you can't grow it.

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 10 = RIR 1-2
Week 11 = RIR 1 (accessories RIR 0-1)
Week 12 = FINAL PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Glute Bridge x15 (3 sec hold at top)
• Quadruped Hip Extension x10/leg
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Hip Thrust x15

1. BARBELL HIP THRUST PAUSED (Rotisserie Pad) ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3 (working sets - don't include primer sets)
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 2-1-1-3
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Heaviest paused thrusts of the program.
LAST SET: rest-pause (rest 15 sec, max reps, rest 15 sec, max reps).

2. DUMBBELL FROG PUMP
Sets: 2
Reps: 25-30
Tempo: 1-0-1-1
Rest: 75 sec
Cues
Soles of the feet together, knees out.
High-rep pump - chase the burn.

3. REVERSE HYPER
Sets: 2
Reps: 15-20
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
Rest: 75 sec
Cues
No swinging - lift with the glutes.
Pause at the top.

4. SINGLE LEG LEG PRESS - HIGH FOOT
Sets: 2
Reps: 12/leg
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Foot high on the platform.
Push through the heel.

5. DB STEP UP (knee-height box)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10/leg
Tempo: 2-0-1-1
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Pull yourself onto the box.
Control the lowering phase.

6. SUPERSET

Reps: 10-12 + hold the last rep wide for 10 sec
Tempo: 1-0-1-2

A2. STRAIGHT ARM CABLE PULLDOWN (upper sprinkle)
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15
Tempo: 2-0-1-1

7. RKC PLANK
Sets: 2
Reps: 20-30 sec
Rest: 45 sec
Cues
Squeeze everything - glutes, quads, fists.
Pull elbows toward toes.

8. FINISHER - HIP THRUST 21s (bodyweight or light band)

No rest within the 21. Rest 60 sec between rounds.
