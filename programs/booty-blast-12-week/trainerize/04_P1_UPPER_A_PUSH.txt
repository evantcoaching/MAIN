UPPER BODY SCULPT A
Vertical Push Priority - Phase 1 (Weeks 1 & 3)
Strength • Width • Shape

SESSION TYPE
Hypertrophy • Stability • Precision

FOCUS
Delts
Upper Back
Shoulder Width
Triceps Definition
*Minimal Chest
Wide shoulders change your silhouette more than any other muscle group - and make the waist look smaller. Train them with intent.

TEMPO STRUCTURE
Every working set follows:
Lower → Bottom Pause → Lift → Top Pause
Default Tempo: 3-1-2-1

OBJECTIVE
Begin with a heavy overhead press, then progress into shoulder width, upper back stability and triceps. Stable shoulders produce stronger lifts.

WARM UP
• Band External Rotation x12
• Scap Push Ups x10
• Light Dumbbell Press x10

BLOCK A | PRIMARY COMPOUND PUSH
A1. Dumbbell Shoulder Press (neutral grip)
3 x 6-8
Tempo 3-1-2-1
Rest 2 min
Cue
Brace your core.
Drive straight overhead.

BLOCK B | UPPER BACK STABILITY
B1. Underhand Lat Pulldown
2 x 10
Rest 90 sec

B2. Seated Rotational Row (SS)
2 x 10-12
↓
B3. Cable Face Pull
2 x 12-15
Rest 90 sec after both
Cue
Pull elbows apart.
Finish with shoulders down.

BLOCK C | SHOULDER WIDTH (Giant Set)
C1. Dual Cable Lateral Raise (behind body)
2 x 12-15
↓
C2. Lean Away Lateral Raise
2 x 10-12/arm
↓
C3. Bottom Partials
10-15 reps
Rest 90 sec
Cue
Lead with the elbows.
Keep tension throughout.

BLOCK D | REAR DELTS
D1. Bent Over Cable Reverse Fly
2 x 12-15
Rest 60 sec
Cue
Think wide.
Pause every rep.

BLOCK E | TRICEPS (Tri-Set)
E1. Overhead Cable Extension
2 x 12-15
↓
E2. Rope Pushdown
2 x 12-15
Rest 75 sec
↓
E3. FUN FINISHER - Cable Skullcrusher
1 x 100 reps AMRAP (light weight - log how many breaks you take and beat it next time)
Cue
Control the lowering.
Lock out every rep.

BLOCK F | ABS
F1. Cable Crunch
2 x 12-15
Rest 45 sec
Cue
Ribs to pelvis.

RECOVERY
2 min deep nasal breathing, long exhale.

SESSION COMPLETE
Great physiques aren't built from random workouts.
They're built through consistent execution, technical mastery and relentless effort.
Stay patient. Stay disciplined. The results will follow.
