FRIDAY BOOTY BLAST - POWER
Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4)
Vertical Hip Extension • Strength • Force Production • Progressive Overload
(Hardest session of the week - 2 full days of recovery on the weekend)

FOCUS
Today is the pinnacle of the training week. With two full recovery days ahead, this session is designed to maximise force production through vertical hip extension while accumulating quality volume across the entire glute complex.
Heavy loading first, accessory work second, glute isolation third, core finisher, metabolic burnout, then a brief recovery reset before you leave.

TODAY'S INTENT
Today's work builds tomorrow's physique. Attack every set with purpose, embrace the challenge and earn your recovery. LFG!

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 1 = RIR 3 (find your weights)
Week 2 = RIR 2
Week 3 = RIR 1-2
Week 4 = PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP ACTIVATION (2 Rounds)
• Band Squat x10
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Zig Zag Band Walk x20 steps
• Bodyweight Box Squat x10
Then ramp up sets on the Belt Squat (not counted).

1. BELT SQUAT (WIDE SUMO STANCE) ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 2.5 min
Cues
Sit as deep as mobility allows.
Push the knees out and drive through the heels.
Stand tall and squeeze at the top.

2. BARBELL HIP THRUST - HEAVY (Rotisserie Pad)
Sets: 3
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 2-0-1-1
Rest: 2.5 min
Cues
Posteriorly tilt the pelvis before driving up.
Shins vertical at the top.
Own the lockout.

3. BULGARIAN SPLIT SQUAT
Sets: 2
Reps: 8-10/leg
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Maintain a long stride.
Slight forward torso lean to bias the glutes.
Drive through the front heel.

4. REVERSE RDL MACHINE
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 3-1-1-1
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Push the hips back into the stretch.
Finish with the glutes, not the lower back.

5. SUPERSET
A1. GLUTE MEDIUS HIP THRUST (band above knees - push knees out at top)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
↓
A2. HIP ABDUCTION MACHINE - HEAVY
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 1-0-1-2
Rest: 60 sec after both
Cues
Drive the knees out as far as possible.
Pause for 2 sec at peak contraction.

6. GLUTE BURNOUT FINISHER
Weeks 1-2 = 1 Round / Weeks 3-4 = 2 Rounds
• Banded Clams x15/side
• Banded Donkey Kicks x20/leg
• Banded Hip Abductions x40
Minimal rest between exercises.
Intent: chase the burn, keep constant tension, completely fatigue the glutes.

7. MACHINE CRUNCH
Sets: 2
Reps: 8-12 (maximum challenging weight)
Tempo: Controlled
Rest: 60 sec
Cues
Curl the rib cage toward the pelvis.
Brace hard and avoid pulling with the hips.

8. RECOVERY RESET - LEGS ELEVATED AGAINST WALL
Duration: 2 min
Slow nasal breathing.
Allow the heart rate to recover.
Promote venous return and begin the recovery process before leaving.

REFUEL - protein + carbs within the next couple of hours.
