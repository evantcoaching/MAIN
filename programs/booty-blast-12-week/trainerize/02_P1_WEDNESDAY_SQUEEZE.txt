BOOTY BLAST WEDNESDAY - SQUEEZE
Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4)
Horizontal Hip Extension • Peak Contraction • Mind-Muscle Connection

FOCUS
Today the glutes work hardest at the TOP of every rep - the fully contracted position. Hip thrusts, bridges and back extensions load the glutes horizontally, where they are most active. Lighter than Friday, but every rep should feel like your glutes are doing ALL the work. This is the session that builds shape and the "shelf".

TODAY'S INTENT
Squeeze with purpose. Feel every rep. If you can't feel it, you can't grow it.

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 1 = RIR 3 (find your weights)
Week 2 = RIR 2
Week 3 = RIR 1-2
Week 4 = PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Glute Bridge x15 (3 sec hold at top)
• Quadruped Hip Extension x10/leg
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Hip Thrust x15

1. BARBELL HIP THRUST PAUSED (Rotisserie Pad) ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3 (working sets - don't include primer sets)
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 2-1-1-3
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Tuck your pelvis before every rep.
Chin to chest, eyes forward.
Finish with a hard 3-second squeeze.

2. KAS GLUTE BRIDGE (back on bench, top half only)
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15
Tempo: 1-0-1-2
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Hips never drop below bench height.
Constant tension.
Glutes, not hammys.

3. 45° BACK EXTENSION - GLUTE BIAS
Sets: 2
Reps: 15-20
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
Rest: 75 sec
Cues
Feet turned out, round your upper back.
Push hips into the pad.
Squeeze glutes to lift - stop at a straight line (no lower back arch).

4. GOBLET SQUAT - 1.5 REPS
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 2-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Down, halfway up, back down, all the way up = 1 rep.
Knees out.
Squeeze glutes to stand.

5. DB WALKING LUNGE - LONG STRIDE
Sets: 2
Reps: 10/leg
Tempo: 2-0-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Long stride, slight forward lean.
Push through the front heel and squeeze at the top.

6. SUPERSET
A1. HIP ABDUCTION MACHINE - 3 WAY
Sets: 2
Reps: 8 lean back + 8 upright + 8 lean forward (no rest)
Tempo: 1-0-1-2
↓
A2. CABLE FACE PULL (upper sprinkle)
Sets: 2
Reps: 15
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
Rest: 60 sec after both
Cues
Abduction: pause wide on every rep.
Face pull: pull to the eyebrows, elbows high, thumbs back.

7. WEIGHTED DEAD BUG
Sets: 2
Reps: 8-10/side
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 45 sec
Cues
Low back glued to the floor.
Exhale fully as the leg extends.

8. FINISHER - BANDED GLUTE BRIDGE 100
100 banded glute bridges for time (rest as needed).
Knees pushed out against the band. Beat last week's time.

TOTAL = 13 WORKING SETS FOR GLUTES
