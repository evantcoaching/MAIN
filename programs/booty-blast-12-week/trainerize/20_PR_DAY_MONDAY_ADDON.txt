★ TODAY'S PR LIFT = BARBELL RDL - 8 REP MAX ★

VALID REP: bar to mid-shin or lower with a neutral spine, 3 sec lowering, hips fully locked out at the top.

HOW TO RUN IT (replaces your 3 RDL working sets)
1. Ramp up: 10 reps light → 6 @ ~50% → 4 @ ~70% → 2 @ ~85% of your target (rest 90 sec - 2 min)
2. PR attempt: 8 clean reps. Up to 3 attempts, 3-4 min rest. Stop as soon as a rep breaks form.
3. Back-off set: drop 15-20%, 1 set to RIR 1. Log it.

REST OF THE SESSION
Run as normal, but do ONE LESS SET on any exercise with 3 sets.
