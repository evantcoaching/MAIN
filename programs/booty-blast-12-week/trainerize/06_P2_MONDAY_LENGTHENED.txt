BOOTY BLAST MONDAY - LENGTHENED
Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8)
Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy

FOCUS
New phase, new variations, heavier loading. We're still loading the glutes in their most lengthened position - the main lift (RDL) stays the same so you can keep building on it. Slow the lowering phase, own the bottom position and make every rep count.

TODAY'S INTENT
Control the stretch. Own the tension. Growth happens where ya willing to stay uncomfortable.

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 5 = RIR 2 (learn the new exercises)
Week 6 = RIR 1-2
Week 7 = RIR 1 + drop set on the abduction
Week 8 = PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Reverse Hypers x10
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Split Squat x6/leg (3 sec down)
• Hip Hinge with Dowel x10

1. BARBELL RDL ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3 (working sets - don't include warm-up sets)
Reps: 6-8
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 2.5 min
Cues
Heavier now - same 3 sec lowering, same depth.
Bar stays on the legs.
Drive the hips through and squeeze.

2. SMITH MACHINE SQUAT - WIDE STANCE, DEEP
Sets: 3
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Feet wide and slightly forward.
Sit down between the heels.
Push the knees out and drive through the heels.

3. FRONT FOOT ELEVATED SPLIT SQUAT (DB)
Sets: 2
Reps: 8-10/leg
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Front foot on a plate/step.
Sink deep - back knee kisses the floor.
Slight forward lean.

4. SMITH HIP THRUST - 1.5 REPS
Sets: 3
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 2-1-1-1
Rest: 2 min
Cues
Down, halfway up, back down, all the way up = 1 rep.
Keep tension in the bottom.
Tuck pelvis, chin down.

5. SUPERSET
A1. STANDING CABLE HIP ABDUCTION
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15/leg
Tempo: 2-1-1-2
↓
A2. INCLINE DB Y RAISE (upper sprinkle)
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15
Tempo: 2-1-1-1
Rest: 60 sec after both
Cues
Abduction: start with the working leg crossed behind - full stretch. Lead with the heel. Week 7 = drop set on the last set.
Y Raise: thumbs up, reach long.

6. HANGING LEG RAISE
Sets: 2
Reps: To technical failure
Rest: 60 sec
Cues
Curl the pelvis up at the top.
No swinging.

7. FINISHER - SLED PUSH
6 x 20m
Rest 40 sec between
Low hips, drive through the balls of the feet.

TOTAL = 13 WORKING SETS FOR GLUTES
