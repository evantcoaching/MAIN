BOOTY BLAST REMIX - MONDAY
Week 9 (Remix Week only)
Lengthened • Something New

FOCUS
One week of new movements to keep things fresh while your body consolidates 8 weeks of hard work. Same goal - load the glutes in the stretch - different tools.

TODAY'S INTENT
Learn it, own it, enjoy it. RIR 2 on everything. No failure, no PRs.

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Reverse Hypers x10
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Cossack Squat x5/side

1. KETTLEBELL DEADLIFT TO SWING
Sets: 3
Reps: 3 deadlifts + 10 swings
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Hinge, don't squat.
Snap the hips, squeeze the glutes, let the bell float.

2. LANDMINE SQUAT (wide)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Hug the bar to the chest.
Sit deep between the heels.

3. COSSACK / LATERAL LUNGE (DB goblet)
Sets: 2
Reps: 8/side
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 75 sec
Cues
Sit back into the working hip.
Keep the heel down.

4. SINGLE LEG HIP THRUST OFF A BOX (feet elevated)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12/leg
Tempo: 2-1-1-2
Rest: 60 sec
Cues
Foot on a box = bigger range.
Tuck the pelvis, squeeze at the top.

5. SUPERSET
A1. BANDED MONSTER WALK
Sets: 2
Reps: 20 steps forward + 20 back
↓
A2. BANDED FIRE HYDRANT
Sets: 2
Reps: 15/leg
Rest: 60 sec after both

6. AB - BEAR CRAWL HOLD + SHOULDER TAPS
Sets: 2
Reps: 20 taps
Rest: 45 sec

7. BOOTY GAMES (partners)
6 min: Partner A holds a bodyweight glute bridge while Partner B does 10 KB swings - swap. Most rounds wins.
