BOOTY BLAST WEDNESDAY - SQUEEZE
Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8)
Horizontal Hip Extension • Peak Contraction • Mind-Muscle Connection

FOCUS
The glutes work hardest at the TOP of every rep today. New variations, heavier paused thrusts. Every rep should feel like your glutes are doing ALL the work.

TODAY'S INTENT
Squeeze with purpose. Feel every rep. If you can't feel it, you can't grow it.

EFFORT THIS WEEK
Week 5 = RIR 2 (learn the new exercises)
Week 6 = RIR 1-2
Week 7 = RIR 1 + rest-pause on the last hip thrust set
Week 8 = PR WEEK (see PR Day notes)

WARM UP (2 Rounds)
• Glute Bridge x15 (3 sec hold at top)
• Quadruped Hip Extension x10/leg
• Lateral Band Walks x20 steps
• Bodyweight Hip Thrust x15

1. BARBELL HIP THRUST PAUSED (Rotisserie Pad) ★ PR LIFT
Sets: 3 (working sets - don't include primer sets)
Reps: 8-10
Tempo: 2-1-1-3
Rest: 2 min
Cues
More load, same 3-second squeeze.
Tuck your pelvis before every rep.
Week 7: last set rest-pause (rest 15 sec, max reps, rest 15 sec, max reps).

2. B-STANCE HIP THRUST (Smith or DB)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12/leg
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
Rest: 60 sec
Cues
80% of the weight on the front leg.
Back foot is just a kickstand.

3. CABLE PULL THROUGH
Sets: 2
Reps: 12-15
Tempo: 2-0-1-2
Rest: 75 sec
Cues
Hips back, soft knees.
Snap through and finish with a hard glute lockout.

4. DEFICIT SUMO GOBLET SQUAT (feet on plates)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12
Tempo: 3-1-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Wide stance, toes out.
Knees track over the toes.
Sink deep, squeeze to stand.

5. CURTSY LUNGE (DB)
Sets: 2
Reps: 10/leg
Tempo: 2-0-1-0
Rest: 90 sec
Cues
Step back and across.
Feel the side of the glute.
Push through the front heel.

6. SUPERSET
A1. SIDE LYING HIP RAISE
Sets: 2
Reps: 10-12/side
Tempo: 1-0-1-2
↓
A2. SINGLE ARM HIGH TO LOW CABLE ROW (upper sprinkle)
Sets: 2
Reps: 12/arm
Tempo: 2-0-1-1
Rest: 60 sec after both
Cues
Hip raise: knee on the bench, lift the hips sideways, pause at the top.
Row: elbow to back pocket.

7. PALLOF PRESS
Sets: 2
Reps: 12/side
Tempo: 2-2-1-0
Rest: 45 sec
Cues
Hold 2 sec at full arm extension.
Don't let the cable rotate you.

8. FINISHER - KICKBACK & ABDUCTION BURNOUT
2 Rounds:
• Cable Kickback x15/leg
• Banded Hip Abductions x30
Minimal rest. Chase the burn.

TOTAL = 13 WORKING SETS FOR GLUTES
