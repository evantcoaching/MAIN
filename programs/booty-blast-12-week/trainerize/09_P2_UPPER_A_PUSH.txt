UPPER BODY SCULPT A
Vertical Push Priority - Phase 2 (Weeks 5 & 7)
Strength • Width • Shape

SESSION TYPE
Hypertrophy • Stability • Precision

FOCUS
Delts
Upper Back
Shoulder Width
Triceps Definition
*Minimal Chest
Wide shoulders change your silhouette more than any other muscle group. Train them with intent.

TEMPO STRUCTURE
Lower → Bottom Pause → Lift → Top Pause
Default Tempo: 3-1-2-1

WARM UP
• Band External Rotation x12
• Scap Push Ups x10
• Light Machine Press x10

BLOCK A | PRIMARY COMPOUND PUSH
A1. Machine or Smith Seated Shoulder Press
3 x 6-8
Tempo 3-1-2-1
Rest 2 min
Cue
Brace your core.
Drive straight overhead.

BLOCK B | UPPER BACK STABILITY
B1. Underhand Lat Pulldown
2 x 10
Rest 90 sec

B2. Seated Rotational Row (SS)
2 x 10-12
↓
B3. Cable Face Pull
2 x 12-15
Rest 90 sec after both
Cue
Pull elbows apart.
Finish with shoulders down.

BLOCK C | SHOULDER WIDTH (Giant Set)
C1. Dual Cable Lateral Raise (behind body)
2 x 12-15
↓
C2. Dumbbell Lateral Raise
2 x 10-12
↓
C3. Top Half Partials
10-15 reps
Rest 90 sec
Cue
Lead with the elbows.
Keep tension throughout.

BLOCK D | REAR DELTS
D1. Bent Over Cable Reverse Fly
2 x 12-15
Rest 60 sec
Cue
Think wide.
Pause every rep.

BLOCK E | TRICEPS (Tri-Set)
E1. Single Arm Overhead DB Extension
2 x 12-15/arm
↓
E2. Rope Pushdown
2 x 12-15
Rest 75 sec
↓
E3. FUN FINISHER - Cable Skullcrusher
1 x 100 reps AMRAP (beat your break count from Phase 1)
Cue
Control the lowering.
Lock out every rep.

BLOCK F | ABS
F1. Cable Crunch
2 x 12-15
Rest 45 sec

RECOVERY
2 min deep nasal breathing, long exhale.
