Tempo key

Four numbers, in seconds: Lower – Bottom pause – Lift – Top pause.

3110

= 3 sec down, 1 sec pause, 1 sec up, no pause at the top.

RIR — reps in reserve

RIR 2 = you could have done 2 more good reps.

Technical failure = the next rep would break your form. Stop there.

Each workout tells you how close to failure to train that week.

How to progress

Add weight when you earn it

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set with good form, add weight next session:

  • Barbell & belt squat: +2.5–5 kg
  • Dumbbells: +1–2 kg each hand
  • Machines & cables: +1 pin or plate

Then build the reps back up. Can't add weight? Add a rep, slow the lowering or go a little deeper. Better than last time = progress.

Log every session

Every exercise has log boxes for each week. Write the weight and reps of your best set, e.g. 60 × 10.

No log = no overload. Your log is how you know you're getting stronger.

Intensity techniques

  • Rest-pause: rest 15 sec, max reps, rest 15 sec, max reps
  • Drop set: drop ~25% and go again
  • Lengthened partials: half reps in the stretch after your last full rep
Train with purpose. Focus on quality movement. Embrace progressive overload. Consistency is what builds stronger, fuller, more resilient glutes.

PR WEEK #1 · WEEK 4

★ PR WEEK

Run that week's normal workouts, with one change: each day has one PR lift that replaces its working sets. Every other exercise with 3 sets drops to 2 sets, so you're fresh to perform.

Monday

Barbell RDL

8-rep max

Bar to mid-shin or lower, neutral spine, 3 sec lowering, hips fully locked out.

Wednesday

Barbell Hip Thrust

5-rep max

Shins vertical at the top, pelvis tucked, 1 sec pause at lockout.

Friday

Belt Squat (wide sumo)

5-rep max

Hip crease to knee height or lower, controlled descent, full lockout.

Upper B

Pull-Up

Max reps

Full hang to chin over bar, no kipping. Not there yet? Least assistance for 5 clean reps.

How to run your PR lift

  1. Ramp up: 10 reps light → 6 @ ~50% → 4 @ ~70% → 2 @ ~85% of your target. Rest 90 sec–2 min.
  2. PR attempt: up to 3 attempts at the target reps, 3–4 min rest. Stop as soon as a rep breaks form.
  3. Back-off set: drop 15–20%, one set to RIR 1. Log it.

Make it count

Ring the PR bell. Write it on the board. Record it on your PR sheet at the back of this book.

PR WEEK #2 · WEEK 8

★ PR WEEK

Run that week's normal workouts, with one change: each day has one PR lift that replaces its working sets. Every other exercise with 3 sets drops to 2 sets, so you're fresh to perform.

Monday

Barbell RDL

8-rep max

Bar to mid-shin or lower, neutral spine, 3 sec lowering, hips fully locked out.

Wednesday

Barbell Hip Thrust

5-rep max

Shins vertical at the top, pelvis tucked, 1 sec pause at lockout.

Friday

Belt Squat (wide sumo)

5-rep max

Hip crease to knee height or lower, controlled descent, full lockout.

Upper B

Pull-Up

Max reps

Full hang to chin over bar, no kipping. Not there yet? Least assistance for 5 clean reps.

How to run your PR lift

  1. Ramp up: 10 reps light → 6 @ ~50% → 4 @ ~70% → 2 @ ~85% of your target. Rest 90 sec–2 min.
  2. PR attempt: up to 3 attempts at the target reps, 3–4 min rest. Stop as soon as a rep breaks form.
  3. Back-off set: drop 15–20%, one set to RIR 1. Log it.

Make it count

Ring the PR bell. Write it on the board. Record it on your PR sheet at the back of this book.

FINAL PR WEEK · WEEK 12

★ PR WEEK

Run that week's normal workouts, with one change: each day has one PR lift that replaces its working sets. Every other exercise with 3 sets drops to 2 sets, so you're fresh to perform.

Monday

Barbell RDL

8-rep max

Bar to mid-shin or lower, neutral spine, 3 sec lowering, hips fully locked out.

Wednesday

Barbell Hip Thrust

5-rep max

Shins vertical at the top, pelvis tucked, 1 sec pause at lockout.

Friday

Belt Squat (wide sumo)

5-rep max

Hip crease to knee height or lower, controlled descent, full lockout.

Upper B

Pull-Up

Max reps

Full hang to chin over bar, no kipping. Not there yet? Least assistance for 5 clean reps.

How to run your PR lift

  1. Ramp up: 10 reps light → 6 @ ~50% → 4 @ ~70% → 2 @ ~85% of your target. Rest 90 sec–2 min.
  2. PR attempt: up to 3 attempts at the target reps, 3–4 min rest. Stop as soon as a rep breaks form.
  3. Back-off set: drop 15–20%, one set to RIR 1. Log it.

Make it count

Ring the PR bell. Write it on the board. Record it on your PR sheet at the back of this book.
